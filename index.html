TTD Darshan Autofill

A browser extension that fills your saved devotee details into TTD's online booking forms — Special Entry Darshan, Srivani, Srivari Seva, and Group Seva — so you're not retyping the same Aadhaar numbers and addresses every time.

What it does

Special Entry Darshan (Pilgrim Booking)

Save up to 6 pilgrims once, fill the whole booking table in one click.

Srivani

Add people from your saved directory or on the fly, fill the Srivani ticket form instantly.

Srivari Seva

One detailed applicant form — including photo and document uploads — filled in one click.

Group Seva

Manage a whole team's details in the extension, fill each member's section as you go.

How to install

  1. Download the extension folder (contact us below for the latest build).
  2. Open Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder.
  4. Open the extension icon, save your details, and start filling forms.
The extension does not submit forms or bypass any queue or CAPTCHA — you still review and submit each form yourself. It doesn't guarantee ticket availability or booking success; that's entirely up to TTD's own system.
